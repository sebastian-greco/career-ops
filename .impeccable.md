## Design Context

### Users
Career-Ops is primarily a personal job-search command center for a single candidate. It is used both at a desk and in short, attention-limited mobile sessions—for example, checking an application, opening an evaluation report, or changing a pipeline status from a phone on the same Wi-Fi.

### Brand Personality
Calm, capable, and candid. The interface should create confidence and momentum without feeling corporate, gamified, or like a developer console.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined, light-first editorial utility: warm neutral surfaces, restrained orange accents, strong typography, and status colors used as information rather than decoration. Preserve dark-mode compatibility. Avoid terminal/TUI mimicry, dense desktop tables on narrow screens, decorative dashboards, and generic card-grid styling.

### Design Principles
1. Make the next useful action obvious: one tap opens a report; status changes happen beside the application.
2. Adapt by context: compact data tables may remain on desktop, while phones get a true single-column touch interface.
3. Keep every primary touch target at least 44px and never depend on hover, double-click, or keyboard shortcuts.
4. Use progressive disclosure for secondary metadata; do not keep a persistent detail sidebar competing with the pipeline.
5. Preserve place and momentum with clear back navigation, stable filters, immediate feedback, and concise labels.
